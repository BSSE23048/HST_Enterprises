1. Show **“Availability on enquiry”**; do not assert real-time stock or quantities.
2. No prices, shopping cart, checkout, or payment flow. Each card should link to a prefilled WhatsApp RFQ and optionally an email RFQ.
3. Use brand names only where the owner’s list names the brand; brand-unspecified entries must remain unbranded until confirmed.
4. Treat original entries 19 and 44 as *potentially overlapping*, not definitely identical; their adjustment ranges differ.
5. Original entry 107 is unbranded, unlike 24/67, so keep separate until brand confirmed.
6. Research exact model specifications and official imagery before publishing manufacturer datasheets, breaking capacities, dimensions or product photos. Verify image reuse rights.
7. Verify whether original 'mm' means **mm²** for conductor cross-sectional areas; the standardized units here are editorial interpretations, not confirmed labels.
8. Keep existing indexed SEO category URLs intact; introduce catalogue categories as filters or sections, with canonical strategy planned before adding new URLs.
